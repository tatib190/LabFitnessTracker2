# Lab: Monthly Salary Calculator
This program calculates the salary of either a full-time or part-time employee by reading their amount of hours and hourly rate, and for full-time employees it adds their bonus.
## How to Run
1. Open the program on IDE.
2. Enter if you're full-time or part-time then enter your hours and hourly rates.
3. View the calculated monthly salary.
